# Prompt Engineering Basics

## Introduction
A prompt is the instruction, question or information given to an AI system. Prompt engineering is the practice of writing and improving prompts to get more useful responses.

## Key Concepts
1. **Task:** State what the AI should do, such as explain, summarise or compare.
2. **Context:** Give background information that helps the AI understand the situation.
3. **Specificity:** Include enough detail to reduce ambiguity.
4. **Output format:** Say whether you want bullets, a table, steps or a short paragraph.
5. **Examples:** Provide an example of the answer style you want when useful.

## Simple Example
Basic: "Write about solar energy."
Improved: "Explain solar energy to a school student in about 120 words. Include two benefits and one limitation."

The improved prompt gives the audience, length and content requirements.

## Practical Applications
Prompt writing is useful for learning, summarising, drafting, brainstorming and coding assistance.
