# Generative AI and Large Language Models

## Introduction
Generative AI refers to systems that create new content based on patterns learned from data. Large Language Models (LLMs) are generative AI models designed to work with language.

## Key Concepts
1. **Generative AI:** Systems that produce text, images, audio, video or code.
2. **LLM:** A model trained on large amounts of text to recognise and generate language.
3. **Prompt:** The instruction or question a user gives an AI system.
4. **Tokens:** Small pieces of text processed by a language model. A token may be a word, part of a word or punctuation.
5. **Hallucination:** When an AI generates information that sounds plausible but is incorrect or unsupported.

## Simple Examples
- Asking an assistant to summarise a paragraph.
- Generating a first draft of an email.
- Asking an LLM to explain a Python error.

## Practical Applications
Generative AI can support writing, brainstorming, tutoring, coding assistance and summarisation. Human review remains important.
