# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## Introduction
Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science focused on building systems that perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognising images or making recommendations.

## Key Concepts
1. **AI systems:** Software that performs tasks such as answering questions, recognising patterns or making predictions.
2. **Narrow AI:** AI designed for a specific task, such as face recognition. Most AI used today is narrow AI.
3. **Machine learning:** An approach in which systems learn patterns from data rather than relying only on fixed instructions.
4. **Generative AI:** AI that creates content such as text, images, audio or code.
5. **AI limitations:** AI can make mistakes, reflect bias or produce confident-sounding but incorrect answers.

## Simple Examples
- An email service identifying spam.
- A map application suggesting a route.
- An AI assistant drafting an email from instructions.

## Practical Applications
AI is used in education, healthcare support, banking, transport, customer service and entertainment recommendations.
