# Introduction to AI Fluency

## Introduction
AI fluency means being able to work with AI systems thoughtfully and effectively. It involves knowing when to use AI, communicating clearly and checking its output.

## Key Concepts
1. **Understanding capabilities:** Knowing what an AI tool can and cannot do.
2. **Clear communication:** Giving relevant context and describing the desired result.
3. **Critical thinking:** Reviewing AI responses instead of accepting every answer.
4. **Responsible use:** Considering privacy, fairness, safety and possible consequences.
5. **Human judgement:** Keeping people involved in decisions that need experience or accountability.

## Simple Examples
- Asking AI to explain a topic, then checking it against course material.
- Avoiding passwords or private information in an unapproved tool.
- Using AI to brainstorm while making the final choice yourself.

## Practical Applications
AI fluency is useful in education, workplaces, research and everyday tasks.

## My Understanding
