# Communicating and Collaborating with AI

## Introduction
Good collaboration with AI starts with a clear task. Users can improve responses by explaining their goal, providing context and describing the format they need.

## Key Concepts
1. **State the goal:** Explain what you want the AI to do.
2. **Provide context:** Include details that affect the answer, such as your skill level or audience.
3. **Set constraints:** Mention limits such as length, tone or topics to avoid.
4. **Review the response:** Check whether it meets the request and whether its claims are reliable.
5. **Refine with follow-up:** Ask a more specific question or request a revision if needed.

## Simple Example
Vague: "Explain Python."
Clearer: "Explain Python lists to a beginner, use a small code example and give three practice questions."

## Practical Applications
These habits help with learning, drafting, brainstorming, summarising and coding assistance.
