# Introduction to AI Agents

## Introduction
An AI agent is a system that receives information, works toward a goal and takes actions. Depending on its design, it may use a model, tools and stored information.

## Key Concepts
1. **Goal:** The task the agent is expected to achieve.
2. **Input:** Information received from a user or its environment.
3. **Decision-making:** Choosing what to do next based on the goal and available information.
4. **Action:** Something the agent does, such as searching a knowledge source or calling a function.
5. **Feedback:** Information received after an action that can guide the next step.

## Simple Example
A study assistant receives "Help me revise machine learning." It identifies the goal, selects relevant study material, offers a quiz and uses the learner's response to suggest a next step.

## Practical Applications
Agents can support research, customer service, scheduling, information retrieval and repetitive workflows. Their permissions should be limited appropriately.

## My Understanding
I understand an AI agent as a system that can work toward a goal by choosing and carrying out actions. Its level of autonomy depends on its design.
